Will it work with my MIDI controller?

If your controller sends standard MIDI messages to Windows, you can usually map its buttons, pads, knobs, faders, encoders and jog wheels in MIDI Command Studio.

Use MIDI Command Studio free to test it with your own device.

  • Pads
  • Knobs
  • Faders
  • Encoders
  • Jog wheels
